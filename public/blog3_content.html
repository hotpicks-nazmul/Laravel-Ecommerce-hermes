Cooking Tips from Hamko Kitchen Experts

\n\n

To help you get the most from your Hamko cookware, here are some expert tips:

\n\n

Pressure Cooking Tips

\n

Preheat Before Adding Oil: Always preheat your pressure cooker before adding oil. This prevents food from sticking and ensures even cooking.

\n\n

Don't Overfill: Never fill your pressure cooker more than two-thirds full. For foods that expand (like rice and beans), don't exceed half capacity.

\n\n

Release Pressure Naturally: For most dishes, allow pressure to release naturally rather than using the quick-release method. This ensures even cooking and prevents toughening of meat.

\n\n

Stainless Steel Pan Tips

\n

The Water Test: To check if your pan is properly preheated, add a few drops of water. If they form a single bead that dances across the surface, your pan is ready.

\n\n

Don't Move Food Too Early: When searing meat, let it develop a crust before attempting to flip. The meat will release naturally when it's properly seared.

\n\n

Deglaze for Flavor: After searing, add wine, broth, or water to the hot pan to deglaze. This lifts all the flavorful browned bits and creates a delicious sauce base.

\n\n

Casserole Cooking Tips

\n

Brown First: Always brown your meat before slow cooking. This develops deep flavors through the Maillard reaction that can't be achieved through slow cooking alone.

\n\n

Layer Ingredients: Place harder vegetables at the bottom and more delicate ingredients on top. This ensures everything cooks evenly.

\n\n

Low and Slow: Casserole dishes benefit from low, slow cooking. Resist the temptation to increase heat—patience yields the best results.

\n\n

Maintaining Your Hamko Cookware

\n\n

Proper care ensures your Hamko cookware lasts for generations:

\n\n

Cleaning: Allow cookware to cool slightly before cleaning. Use warm water and mild detergent for daily cleaning. For stubborn stains, create a paste with baking soda and water.

\n\n

Storage: Store cookware in a dry place. If stacking, place a soft cloth between pieces to prevent scratching.

\n\n

Maintenance: For stainless steel, occasional polishing with a dedicated stainless steel cleaner will maintain the mirror finish. Avoid abrasive cleaners that can scratch the surface.
